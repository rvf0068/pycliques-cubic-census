# Provenance and conventions

The CSVs are transcriptions of the project files updated_behavior_ledger.csv and cubic14_complete_homotopy_transitions.csv (26 November 2026 snapshot). Headers, IDs, sources and values are retained. The order of disconnected rows differs: join on **id**, not index. The zero-based index is local to each ledger. Connected IDs are one-based line numbers of the fixed data/cubic14.g6. Graph6 encodes the cubic graph C; the studied graph is G = complement(C).

Disconnected IDs: 4+10:j for the disjoint union of K4 and connected cubic j of order 10; 6+8:i:j for order-six graph i and connected cubic j of order eight; 4+4+6:i for two K4 factors and order-six graph i. Here i=1 is K3,3, i=2 is the triangular prism.

Connected datasets are exact copies from the pyg6data package in [pycliques-project](https://github.com/rvf0068/pycliques-project), originally sourced from [House of Graphs](https://houseofgraphs.org/Cubic). See data/manifest.json for Git blob identities. Cite Coolsaet, D'hondt and Goedgebeur, *House of Graphs 2.0*, Discrete Applied Mathematics 325 (2023), 97–107.

Behavior: DIVERGENT, CONVERGENT (all 18 recorded cases pare to a point), UNRESOLVED_IN_LEDGER. Stage refers to the pared iterate P_0 = pare(G), P_{j+1}=pare(K(P_j)); some positives were found on the unpared precursor. Source is a historical discovery route, not a current runtime status. Target is the archived description. Four earlier_archived_certificate_not_replayed records originally lacked witnesses; fresh certificates now establish all four (150, 252, 270, 472). The source labels are retained as historical provenance, but none of these records is still historical-only. The repository now has independently replayable divergence witnesses for all 502 positive records: 396 at stage zero, 72 at stage one, 33 stage-two coaffine witnesses, and graph 308's separate cut-retraction certificate. The 18 convergent records have point-paring certificates. Thus all 520 determined behavior records have a repository replay path. The 20 remaining records are unresolved. All topology profiles are reported as exact wedges; their individual homotopy certificates are not bundled in this import. Empty JSON object means a point. The topology ledger's same compares the recorded exact wedge profiles.

The JSON for 308 is the saved certificate with raw clique IDs and support masks. Six raw IDs are folded out to obtain the 571-vertex host. The eleven formerly unbundled stage-two cases were recovered by a deeper native search and an implicit incidence-cut search. See the two run records in research/ and the graph-by-graph Org workbook for host orders, seeds, and methods. The incidence search constructs the pared P2 host implicitly from P1, verifies the paring, then checks an ordinary retraction onto O_d; special octahedra are included as a special case of such retractions. No complete updated order-12 ledger has been recovered.

Literature: R. Villarroel-Flores, [On the clique behavior and Hellyness of the complements of regular graphs](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2022.113230), Discrete Mathematics 346 (2023), 113230; F. Larrión, V. Neumann-Lara, M. A. Pizaña, *On expansive graphs*, European Journal of Combinatorics 30 (2009), 372–379.

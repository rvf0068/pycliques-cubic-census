# Certificate bundles

- **coaffine_order14_stage0.json**: 396 fresh finite coaffine maps, for every recorded depth-zero divergence.
- **coaffine_order14_stage1.json**: 72 fresh finite coaffine maps, for every recorded depth-one divergence.
- **308.json**: the recovered depth-two cut and retraction witness, independently replayed by the existing validator.

The coaffine bundles were regenerated by a finite-domain search with weak-edge constraints, arc consistency, and an expansive-leaf acceptance test. They are newly constructed witnesses, **not recovered copies of the original unavailable archive**. Their targets need not coincide with the historical target descriptions. Historical source and target columns are unchanged.

For each ID, the stage is relative to the deterministic pared sequence: complement the cubic input, fold the least removable vertex (choosing the least dominator), relabel survivors in increasing old-label order, and repeat after constructing the clique graph with maximal completes sorted lexicographically by increasing vertex tuples. Map entries are images of consecutive host vertices. Factors are lists of host vertices. The verifier checks the full morphism, coaffinity, stable image, its explicit power retraction, and nonempty pairwise completely joined invariant factors (at least three, or two with one connected).

The mathematical justification is the expansive summand criteria and admissible inclusion for coaffined graphs, in Larrión, Neumann-Lara and Pizaña, *On expansive graphs*, European Journal of Combinatorics 30 (2009), 372–379, Theorems 2.4, 3.6 and 4.6. See the survey for the argument via stable images and retractions. A coaffine endomorphism alone is not a divergence certificate.

```sh
uv run --with networkx python scripts/validate_census.py

```

Together these commands replay 469 divergent and 18 clique-null cases, a total of 487 behavior certificates. Thirty-three further recorded divergence verdicts are not yet replayable from this repository; this includes the four historical-only cases. Twenty cases remain unresolved in the research ledger. No topology witness is established merely by comparing the imported homotopy records.
